# Premium

Documentation for premium CodeRx data marts. These pages are unlisted and not indexed by search engines.

## Pricing

- [Pricing - NADAC Historical](/premium/pricing-nadac-historical) - Historical NADAC acquisition cost per NDC, one row per price period.
- [Pricing - CMS ASP HCPCS](/premium/pricing-cms-asp-hcpcs) - Current Medicare Part B ASP pricing for HCPCS J codes.
- [Pricing - CMS ASP HCPCS Historical](/premium/pricing-cms-asp-hcpcs-historical) - Historical (SCD2) CMS ASP pricing for HCPCS J codes.

## Drug Data

- [Brand / Generic Availability](/premium/brand-generic-availability) - Brand and generic availability per ingredient and dose form.
- [Packaging](/premium/packaging) - Package-level dimensional information derived from FDA packaging components.
- [Label Images](/premium/label-images) - DailyMed drug label images mapped to NDCs.
- [Indications](/premium/indications) - Therapeutic indications linking clinical drugs to diseases (MeSH / ICD10CM).

## NCPDP Crosswalks

- [NCPDP - Representative NDCs](/premium/ncpdp-representative-ndcs) - A single representative NDC chosen for each RxNorm drug product.
- [NCPDP - Dose Forms](/premium/ncpdp-dose-forms) - Crosswalk between RxNorm dose forms and NCPDP/NCIt terminology.
- [NCPDP - DEA Schedules](/premium/ncpdp-dea-schedules) - Crosswalk between DEA schedules and NCPDP/NCIt terminology.
- [NCPDP - Dose Units](/premium/ncpdp-dose-units) - NCPDP DoseUnitOfMeasure terminology from the NCPDP/NCIt vocabulary.

## REMS

- [REMS - Main](/premium/rems-main) - Active FDA REMS programs with their currently approved version.
- [REMS - Packages](/premium/rems-packages) - Package-level NDC mappings for active REMS programs.
- [REMS - Drugs](/premium/rems-drugs) - RxNorm drug mappings for active REMS programs.
